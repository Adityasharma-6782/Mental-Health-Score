About Signal

What this tool does, how the score is modeled, and where it stops.

01 · Why we built this

A quick, honest read on how you're doing

Students juggle screen time, coursework, sleep, and stress every single day — and most of us never stop to look at how those pieces fit together. Mental Health Signal takes a handful of everyday habits and turns them into a single, easy-to-read score, so you can notice patterns before they turn into something bigger.

02 · How the score works

Twelve inputs, one signal

The model looks at your age, academic level, and country alongside your digital habits — screen time, phone unlocks, platform, and purpose of use — plus your lifestyle basics: study hours, physical activity, sleep, and how stressed you feel day to day. These are fed into a trained model that outputs a score from 0 to 10.

1

You fill in your profile & habits

A short form covering academics, digital habits, and lifestyle.

2

The model reads the pattern

Your inputs are compared against patterns learned from real survey data.

3

You get a score, instantly

A 0–10 read plus a plain-language band: strained, balanced, or strong.

03 · What this isn't

Not a diagnosis — a mirror

This score is modeled from statistical patterns, not clinical evaluation. It can't detect specific conditions and it isn't a substitute for talking to a doctor, counselor, or therapist. Think of it as a nudge to pay attention, not a verdict.

04 · Your data

Private by default

Your account and submissions are tied only to you. We don't sell or share your data, and you're free to stop using the tool at any time.
